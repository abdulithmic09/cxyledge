Privacy policy

Cxyledge · last updated 21/10/2026

Cxyledge is a cylinder inventory, billing and company management app. This page explains what information it keeps and how it is used.

What we store

  • Your account: your name and email address. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We do not receive your Google password.
  • Your business records: the entries, companies, cylinder serial numbers, payments, bills, letterhead details and files you or your team add to a book.

How it is used

Your information is used only to sign you in and to show your book to you and the people you share it with. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.

Where it is kept

Data is stored in a hosted Postgres database (Neon) and the app runs on Vercel. Both are used only to run Cxyledge.

Who can see it

A book is visible only to the people who belong to it: its creator and anyone who joined with its share code.

Deleting your data

To delete your account or a book, email abadansari810@gmail.com and we will remove it.
